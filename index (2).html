What it says

    The score is the model's output, not a calibrated probability. It is evidence, not proof: polished human writing can score high.

    How it works

    1. Real and AI-written reviews47,896 genuine reviews from Amazon Reviews 2023, plus 17,453 written by three open LLMs, matched in star rating and length.
    2. A small transformer learns the differenceMiniLM fine-tuned, then tested on products, and on one LLM, that it never saw in training.
    3. A second model reads the aspectsDistilled from a DeBERTa aspect-sentiment model, so it can say "battery: negative" from one sentence.
    4. Shrunk to run in a browserBoth exported to ONNX and quantized to 8-bit, then run with Transformers.js on your device.

    Check a product

    Paste an Amazon product link or its ASIN. Reports exist for products whose reviews TrustLens extension users have already opened; the extension shares scores, never review text.

    Use it on Amazon

    The Chrome extension scores every review on a product page, marks the suspicious ones, and shows a rating recomputed from the reviews that read human.

    1. Download trustlens-extension.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
    4. Open an Amazon product page and scroll to the reviews.
    Built by Mahendra Meena as a student project. Training data: Amazon Reviews 2023 (McAuley Lab, UCSD).
