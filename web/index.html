Paste any product review. A model trained on real Amazon reviews and LLM-written ones estimates how likely it is to be AI-generated, and pulls out what it says about battery, build, value and more. It runs entirely in your browser; nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Check a product

Enter an Amazon ASIN (the 10-character code after /dp/ in the product URL). You will see results only for products whose reviews TrustLens extension users have already viewed.

How it works

  1. Real and AI-written reviewsGenuine reviews from the Amazon Reviews 2023 dataset, plus reviews written by several open LLMs, matched in star rating and length.
  2. A small transformer learns the differenceFine-tuned and tested on products, and on an LLM, it never saw during training.
  3. Shrunk to run in a browserExported to ONNX and quantized to 8-bit, then run with Transformers.js on your device.
  4. Shared scores, not textThe extension sends only scores to a Cloudflare Worker, which builds a trust report per product.

Get the Chrome extension

The extension marks each review on an Amazon product page and shows a trust-adjusted rating.

  1. Download trustlens-extension.zip from the latest GitHub release and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open any Amazon product page and scroll to the reviews.
TrustLens is a student research project. Scores are statistical estimates, not proof that a review is fake. Training data: Amazon Reviews 2023 (McAuley Lab, UCSD).
